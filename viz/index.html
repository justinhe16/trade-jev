A run is a backtest that called Jev. A replay re-scores its stored answers under the settings above. Replays are approximate: Jev answered with the run's own positions in its state. Fills use the exact bid/ask at decision time + latency; stops and targets are checked once per second and fill at their own price (no gap slippage), so with stops off P&L matches the Python replay exactly and with tight stops it reads optimistic.
